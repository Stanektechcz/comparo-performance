# Campaign marketplace

Six self-service products, each gated on a plan entitlement rather than a sales conversation:

| Product | Requires | Estimated visibility |
|---|---|---|
| Featured Deal | Pro | ~52k impressions / 30 d |
| Country Spotlight | Pro | ~18k market sessions / 30 d |
| Category Spotlight | Pro | ~41k category sessions / 30 d |
| Newsletter Feature | Growth | 18.4k subscribers, one send |
| Research Sponsorship | Growth | PR reach + backlinks |
| Featured Merchant | Growth | ~184k impressions / 30 d |

## Order flow

Choose campaign → configure (market, dates, budget) → preview creative → submit → admin approval
(with the compliance gate) → schedule → run → report.

Eligibility is checked on three axes before submission is accepted: plan entitlement, inventory
availability for that placement and market, and market compliance. Each failure returns the specific
reason — plan required, capacity booked, or the rule that blocks the market.

## Report transparency

A campaign report separates **paid impressions** from **organic visibility**. The merchant also gets a
separate organic value report, so paid and earned performance are never blended into one number.

## Estimates are labelled

Expected clicks, conversions and revenue on unstarted campaigns are marked as estimates. Delivered
figures come from the campaign's own counters.
