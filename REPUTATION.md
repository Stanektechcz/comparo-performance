# Reputation & badges

Reputation measures useful contribution, not activity volume. Nothing rewards posting for the
sake of posting.

## Points

| Action | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Verified review published | +20 |
| Helpful vote received | +2 |
| Accepted answer | +15 |
| Community guide published | +25 |
| Community deal approved | +10 |
| Confirmed report | +5 |
| New topic posted | +10 |
| Reply posted | +4 |

Nothing is awarded for votes cast, page views, logins or streaks. Reputation is removed when the
content that earned it is rejected or hidden.

## Levels

| Level | Reputation |
| --- | --- |
| New Member | 0 |
| Contributor | 50 |
| Trusted Contributor | 250 |
| Expert Contributor | 700 |
| Top Contributor | 1500 |

Level unlocks capability, not status: Trusted Contributor may submit guides without pre-approval
delays, Expert Contributor may flag content straight into the escalated queue.

## Badges

| Badge | Earned by |
| --- | --- |
| Verified Buyer | A review confirmed by conversion, order reference or manual check |
| Early Member | Joined in the platform's first year |
| Helpful Reviewer | 50+ helpful votes received |
| Top Reviewer | 25+ published reviews with above-average helpfulness |
| Community Expert | 10+ accepted answers |
| Deal Hunter | 5+ approved community deals |
| Forum Contributor | 25+ replies that were not removed |
| Trusted Member | Over a year old with a clean moderation history |
| Guide Author | A published community guide |

Administrators can define additional badges and grant them manually; manual grants and reputation
adjustments are written to the audit log with the actor and rationale.

## Public vs internal

Reputation, level and badges are public. The internal review trust score and any anti-fraud
signals are not, and are never exposed through the API. Users can hide profile components
(reviews, topics, deals, badges) or the whole public profile in privacy settings; published
reviews stay attached to products and shops regardless.

## Reputation history

Every change is recorded with the action, delta, source entity and timestamp, and is visible to
the member on their own profile — so a drop after a moderation decision is explainable rather
than mysterious.
