# Newsletter

## Types

Weekly deals · Price watch · Market digest · Community digest · Research digest · Merchant news.

## Segments

All subscribers · Germany · follows a merchant · price watchers · community contributors ·
deal hunters · inactive 30 days. Segmentation uses market, follows, saved products and community
activity — **no sensitive profiling**.

## Builder

Blocks: Hero · Deals · Price drops · Products · Shops · Reviews · Community · Research. The preview
renders the assembled digest for the selected segment.

## Lifecycle

Draft → Scheduled → Sent, with per-send analytics: sent, open rate, click rate, merchant clicks and
unsubscribes.

## Personalisation

Digests use followed products, followed shops, market and saved items. The price-drop digest is
literally "5 saved products dropped in price" — computed from the reader's own saves, not a generic
blast.

## Re-engagement

The "inactive 30 days" segment can receive a price-drop digest for their saved products. Anyone who
opted out of that category is excluded from the send; opt-outs are per digest, not global, and
`/newsletter` lets a reader subscribe to each one separately with its own unsubscribe.
