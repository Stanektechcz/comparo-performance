# Referrals & engagement points

## Referral

Each account derives a stable code from its identity (`CMP-XXXXX`) plus a shareable link. Tracking:
invites → visits → registrations → activated, with weekly cohorts and an activation rate.

## Rewards

1 invite → Inviter badge · 3 → +50 reputation · 5 → 250 Comparo points · 10 → early access to
price-history exports. Badges, reputation and points only: **no cash, no prize draws, no chance
mechanics.**

## Abuse detection

Self-referrals (shared device fingerprint), rapid signup bursts, and referrer/referee sharing a
session are detected; rewards are withheld, quarantined or voided and the case is logged.

## Comparo points

Earned for approved reviews with photos, accepted data corrections, helpful answers, activated
referrals, approved community deals and confirmed coupon reports.

**Points never raise a Trust Score and never influence ranking.** Trust is computed from verification
and behaviour; engagement is a separate axis on purpose, so participation can never be bought or
farmed into credibility.
