# How a shop gets noticed

Eleven rungs, **ordered by how much they interrupt a shopper** rather than by what they cost.

| Rung | What | Price | Intrusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fix your data — feed attributes, prices, stock, EANs | free | 0 |
| 2 | Declare your dosing | free | 0 |
| 3 | Earn the delivery labels | free | 0 |
| 4 | Answer people — reviews, Q&A, announcements | free | 1 |
| 5 | Shop desk hour in a market room | €49 | 1 |
| 6 | Answer a demand signal with a public price commitment | free | 1 |
| 7 | Run deals | €29 | 2 |
| 8 | Native comparison card, after the organic table | €900 | 3 |
| 9 | Category or market featured card | €1,400 | 4 |
| 10 | Homepage featured | €2,400 | 4 |
| 11 | Market launch package | €3,900 | 5 |

The first six cost nothing or under €100 a month, and they are the ones we push, because they
improve what a shopper is actually reading. Each rung publishes what it does (with a measured
effect where we have one) and what it cannot do.

**The intrusion scale** runs Invisible → Passive → Contextual → Declared → Prominent →
Unmissable, and each level is defined in words so a shop can decide how much interruption it is
willing to buy.

**Never for sale at any rung:** a position in the offer table or ComparoRank, any computed label,
a delivery promise the measurement does not support, review visibility or weight, a place in an
editorial comparison or community verdict, presence in a room, group or topic.

The least intrusive paid promotion we sell is the **shop desk hour**: nobody sees it who did not
choose to be in the room, and what it leaves behind is answers — a median of 14 answered
questions and two promoted topics that keep answering for months.
