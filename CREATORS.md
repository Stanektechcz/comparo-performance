# Creator programme

## Creator entity

Name, handle, platform, country, audience size, topic, status, affiliate code, sub-id, clicks,
conversions, revenue, commission, tier, disclosure state and submitted posts.

Pipeline: Discovered → Qualified → Contacted → Interested → Onboarding → Active → Paused → Rejected.

## Qualification (0–100)

Audience relevance (22) + market fit (16) + engagement proxy (18) + content quality (14) +
brand safety (12) + conversion potential (18).

## Tracking

Every creator gets a sub-id (`subid=creator_handle`) and, at tier 2, a coupon code. Performance is
reported as clicks, CVR, EPC, revenue, commission and new users, with a median comparison so a
weak placement is visible rather than assumed.

## Tier review

Above 250 conversions the system suggests a promotion; below 60 % of median CVR on meaningful volume
it suggests reviewing placement quality instead of cutting the creator.

## Disclosure

Mandatory in the content itself. No undisclosed placements, and no requirement to reach a particular
verdict about a product — the data is the pitch.

## Privacy

Commercial metrics are visible to the creator and the growth team only. The leaderboard is
admin-only; nothing commercial is published publicly. `/creators` is the public page and contains no
individual performance data.
