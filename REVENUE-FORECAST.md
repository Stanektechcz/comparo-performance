# Revenue forecast

Three scenarios — Base, Upside, Downside — across subscription, affiliate, sponsored and API revenue.

## Assumptions are published, not buried

* Subscription growth applies the weighted sales pipeline at a stated share of expected annual value.
* Affiliate growth follows the observed 7- and 30-day commission run rate, not a target.
* Sponsored assumes booked campaigns deliver their remaining budget at current pacing.
* API holds the single Business subscriber plus observed overage.
* No paid acquisition, price increase or new market is assumed in any scenario.

## Forecast accuracy

Previous forecast versus actual is tracked so the model can be judged rather than trusted.

## Honesty

The forecast is a deterministic projection from seeded run rates. It is not a commitment, not a
financial statement and not accounting output. The revenue-recognition view is a simple prototype
schedule and claims no accounting compliance.
