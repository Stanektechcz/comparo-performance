# Market expansion

## Market Expansion Score (0–100)

Search demand (18) + merchant count (20) + product coverage (18) + review activity (14) +
community activity (8) + affiliate programmes (12) + SEO coverage (10).

Phases: **Mature** ≥ 75 · **Growth** ≥ 55 · **Opportunity** ≥ 38 · **Early / coverage gap** below.

## Board

Germany — Mature · Czechia — Growth · France — Opportunity · Poland — Growth · Spain — Early ·
Sweden — Coverage gap · United States — Early. Each market shows merchants, products, offers,
reviews, affiliates and progress against its goals.

## Goals per market

Verified merchants · offers · reviews · SEO pages · affiliate partnerships — displayed as progress
bars against target, so "we are in this market" is a measured claim rather than a flag on a map.

## Launch playbook

| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Merchant acquisition — 10 verified, 3 with national coverage | merchants@ |
| SEO — market hub, 50 pages above the quality threshold, hreflang complete | seo@ |
| Community — local category, 5 seeded discussions, 2 ambassadors | community@ |
| Affiliate — 5 live programmes, tracking verified end to end | affiliate@ |
| Newsletter — market segment with a localised weekly digest | content@ |
| Research — one market-specific data story with methodology | content@ |

## Launch checklist

Country config · currency · localisation · compliance rules · merchant coverage · SEO landing pages ·
deals · community hub · affiliate programmes · newsletter segment.

## Coverage gaps

Demand-versus-supply per market feeds the opportunity engine directly: a market scoring below 55
where demand exists produces merchant-acquisition opportunities, not a note in a slide.
