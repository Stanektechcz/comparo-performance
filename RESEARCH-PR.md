# Research & PR

## Data story detector

Largest price drop this month · most competitive market · fastest-improving shop score ·
shipping costs changing · brand availability growth. Stories are detected from internal price,
trust and coverage history — never sourced elsewhere.

## Research story score

Uniqueness · data confidence · market relevance · recency · shareability.

## Pipeline

Idea → Draft → Ready → Pitched → Published.

## Methodology is mandatory

**No data story publishes without a methodology.** Every report states what was measured, how it was
normalised (for example price per 100 g of protein, shipping included), what was excluded and over
what window. A finding we cannot explain is a finding we do not publish.

## Report builder

Compose from market, category, brand, shop and date range. A generated draft carries headline,
summary, methodology, charts, key findings, data table and related entities.

## PR CRM

Publication, contact, market, topic, status and notes. Pipeline: Idea → Draft → Ready → Pitched →
Published → Rejected. The prototype records outreach; it does not send it.

## Backlinks & linkable assets

Manual backlink tracker (source, URL, target page, date, status) and an asset register — research,
data explorer, calculators, charts, reports — with the link count each has earned.

## Social repurposing

One research report generates draft copy for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, short video, community post and
newsletter, each with Draft → Approved → Published. **Nothing is auto-posted externally.**
Shareable data cards are derived strictly from internal data and carry their source line.
