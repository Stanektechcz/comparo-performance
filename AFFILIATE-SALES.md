# Affiliate sales

## CRM

Per merchant: programme status, network, commission model, rate, cookie window, contact, negotiation
stage, revenue, EPC, conversion rate — and a commission history so rate changes are traceable.

Statuses: No programme · Researching · Available · Applied · Approved · Rejected ·
Direct negotiation · Live · Paused.

## Affiliate Opportunity Score (0–100)

Outbound clicks (26) + conversion proxy (18) + user demand (14) + merchant trust (14) +
market coverage (12) + not-yet-monetised bonus (16).

## Non-monetised traffic report

The most important report in this section: merchants receiving substantial outbound clicks with **no
affiliate relationship**. Each row converts to a P0 outreach task. This is revenue already being
delivered for free.

## Low EPC report

Merchants with real traffic and weak earnings per click, with likely causes attached: conversion rate
below 3 %, broken or untracked links on file, or commission below market. Fixing tracking comes
before renegotiating terms.

## Exclusive deal pipeline

Idea → Proposed → Negotiating → Scheduled → Active → Ended, with expected clicks, conversions and
revenue **explicitly labelled as estimates**.

## Firewall

The Commercial Opportunity Score and everything in this section are admin-only and never feed
ComparoRank. Commission terms are not published unless deliberately disclosed, and a merchant can
only ever see their own numbers.
