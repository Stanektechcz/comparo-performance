# Content engine

## Inputs

Search queries · zero-result searches · market gaps · price trends · review trends · community
questions · product relationships · merchant data.

## Opportunity types

Guide · Comparison · Market page · Brand page · Shop comparison · Data report · FAQ ·
Community answer · Research article.

## Content Opportunity Score (0–100)

Search demand (24) + commercial intent (20) + data availability (20) + competition proxy (14) +
internal link potential (12) + freshness (10).

## Quality gate

A page may only reach **Published** when it is `Publishable`: data available **and** score ≥ 60.
Everything else is `Needs data`, `Too thin` or `Park`, and the Advance button refuses with the
reason. Data existing is not a reason to publish — this is the rule that keeps programmatic SEO from
turning into landfill.

## Brief generator

Target intent · primary entity · related entities · suggested title and H1 · questions to answer ·
the unique data Comparo holds · internal links · suggested schema. Briefs are generated from the
entity, not written by hand.

## Statuses

Idea → Brief → Draft → Review → Approved → Published → Needs update.

## Maintenance

* **Internal link tasks** — concrete "add a link from A to B" items with the reason.
* **Refresh queue** — pages quoting stale prices, dead merchants or broken links, ranked by severity.
* **Content value score** — traffic, revenue, engagement, links and freshness, so the assets worth
  protecting are obvious.
* **Content cluster** — pillar, supporting guides, products, comparisons, brands and community
  threads for one topic.

## AI answer gaps

Questions users repeatedly ask that Comparo can answer from structured data, flagged with whether an
answer already exists. Answered questions get FAQ schema and Ask Comparo suggestions; unanswered ones
become pages.
