# Sponsored placements

## Inventory

Eight placements with fixed capacity per market: homepage featured merchant (2), homepage featured
deal (3), category featured merchant (2), country hub featured merchant (2), product related sponsor
(1), deal hub (4), newsletter (1), research (1). Each carries a base price, pricing model
(fixed / CPM / CPC / CPA / hybrid), minimum spend and an audience figure.

Capacity is enforced by an inventory check. When a placement is sold out in a market, approval is
**refused** — there is no opaque auction reshuffling positions behind the scenes.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Every sponsored consumer unit displays **Sponsored**. No exceptions.
2. Sponsorship is not an input to ComparoRank, Trust Score or review scores.
3. No campaign may run in a market where the merchant's products are restricted — the compliance gate
   blocks it. Seeded proof: SP-105 (yohimbine, FR) cannot be approved.
4. UNKNOWN compliance never auto-qualifies for sponsorship; it routes to manual review.
5. Targeting is country, language, category, brand context and page type only. **No health,
   condition, medication or other sensitive targeting, ever.**
6. Frequency cap: 3 sponsored units per session, 8 per day.

## Campaign lifecycle

Draft → Pending Approval → Scheduled → Active → Paused → Completed, or Rejected with a stated reason.
Approval reviews merchant, creative, market, landing page, compliance and budget. Creative carries
headline, description, logo, CTA, destination and its own disclosure line, previewed as it will
appear.

## Delivery and ROI

Impressions, clicks, CTR against a per-placement benchmark, conversions, attributed revenue, spend,
pacing, CPC, CPA and a ROAS proxy. The ROAS note states plainly that it is attributed from tracked
merchant clicks under a last-touch, 30-day model — not the merchant's own reported revenue.

## Sponsored vs exclusive

**Sponsored** means the merchant paid for placement. **Comparo Exclusive** means an offer was
negotiated for Comparo users. An agreement can be both, and then it is labelled as both.
